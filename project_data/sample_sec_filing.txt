Apple Inc. reported total revenue of $394.3 billion for fiscal year 2024, representing a 7% increase compared to 2023. Net income was $101.5 billion. Total assets were $352.8 billion and total liabilities were $279.2 billion. EBITDA was $135.2 billion. Research and development expenses amounted to $29.9 billion. Main risks included supply chain disruptions, regulatory challenges, market competition, economic uncertainty, and dependence on third-party suppliers.
